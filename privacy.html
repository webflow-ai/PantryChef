Privacy Policy & Health Telemetry Standards

PantryChef is engineered for hardworking professionals who demand absolute transparency about their culinary, household, and dietary data. Here is our unambiguous commitment to your privacy.

no_accounts

Zero Ad Tracking

We do not sell, rent, or trade your grocery or dietary habits to advertisers, insurance brokers, or data aggregators.

Ephemeral Vision Scans

Photos of your crisper drawer or sabzi tray are processed solely to extract ingredient labels and discarded immediately.

Encrypted Nutrition Ledger

All family calorie targets and diabetic constraints are encrypted at rest with industry-standard TLS 1.3 / AES-256 protocols.

1. Information We Collect

To provide instantaneous, healthy recipe recommendations strictly calibrated under 30 minutes, PantryChef collects only the minimum information required:

  • Pantry & Fridge Inventory: Items you tap or type (e.g. paneer, spinach, atta, pulses) to generate meals.
  • Dietary & Health Restrictions: Explicit toggles you activate, such as Lacto-Vegetarian, Low-GI / Diabetic, Satvik (no onion/garlic), Gluten-Free, or nut allergies.
  • Household Diners & Calorie Targets: Number of diners (1 to 4+) and daily caloric guardrails (e.g., 450 kcal per plate) stored in your local profile.
  • AI Camera Scans: Images captured via the AI Vision scanner. These images are processed transiently via secure vision APIs and are never used to train generalized models without explicit consent.

2. How Your Data Is Processed

Your data is utilized strictly for autonomous culinary synthesis:

  1. Recipe Synthesis: Translating leftover pantry items into nutritionally balanced recipes that honor user-defined calorie ceilings.
  2. Harmonization Logic: Resolving conflicting household dietary profiles (e.g. diabetic parent + growing child) into a unified single-skillet meal.
  3. Habit Tracking: Synchronizing daily calorie consumption on your Raat Ka Register.

3. Data Retention & Local Storage

By default, PantryChef prioritizes client-side persistence using HTML5 LocalStorage and IndexedDB. Your dabba staples and custom ingredient lists reside on your device. Clearing your browser cache or tapping "Reset Pantry" permanently deletes this information.

4. Compliance & Legal Rights

PantryChef adheres to the India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You possess the unconditional right to access, rectify, export, or delete your nutritional profile at any time.
