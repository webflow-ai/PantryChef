1 Tawa Heat & Tempering (3 mins)
Step 1 of 3

Heat your cast-iron skillet on medium flame. Add 1.5 tsp cold-pressed mustard oil. Crackle cumin seeds (jeera) and 1 slit green chili. Add chopped red onions and ginger-garlic paste. Saute until fragrant and golden translucent.

Ingredients for this step: 1.5 tsp Mustard Oil 1 tsp Jeera 1 Chopped Onion 1 tsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
