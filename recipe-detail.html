Tawa Paneer Bhurji & Multigrain Phulkas

A vibrant cast-iron skillet saute of hand-crumbled soft paneer, caramelized onions, crisp bell peppers, and fresh spinach ribbons, paired with warm whole-wheat phulkas and probiotic cold roasted jeera raita.

Prep Time 6 Mins
Cook Time 12 Mins
Protein 26.5 g
Glycemic Low GI
Household Portion Scaling
Required Pantry Ingredients
  • 200g (crumbled)
  • 1 cup (washed)
  • 120g (for 4 phulkas)
  • 150g (chilled)
  • 1.5 tsp (low oil)
  • Standard Dabba

Step-by-Step Skillet Protocol

Single Skillet Cook
1

Tawa Tempering & Aromatics

3 Mins

Heat a flat iron tawa on medium-high flame with 1 tsp cold-pressed mustard oil. Crackle 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera) and 1 slit green chili. Add finely diced red onions and ginger-garlic paste. Saute for 2.5 minutes until onions turn translucent with caramelized edges.

2

Spinach Fold & Paneer Sear

6 Mins

Toss in the finely shredded palak ribbons, turmeric, and pav bhaji masala. Cook for 60 seconds until spinach wilts. Crumble the paneer by hand directly over the tawa. Sprinkle pink rock salt and toss briskly for 4 minutes so moisture evaporates without making paneer chewy.

3

Puffing Phulkas & Whipping Raita

5 Mins

Roll multigrain dough paper-thin. Toast on hot flame until phulkas balloon completely with zero oil. Whisk chilled dahi with roasted cumin powder and a touch of black salt. Garnish paneer with fresh coriander and serve piping hot.
