## Brand & Style

This design system channels the refined, high-sensory atmosphere of modern Michelin-starred kitchens and avant-garde culinary technology. It balances uncompromising precision with luminous warmth. The aesthetic pairs deep obsidian and carbon foundations with radiant saffron, gold leaf, and cultured amber accents.

### Design Principles
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Visual surfaces mimic polished granite counters, matte chef pans, and low-lit cellar tasting rooms. Contrast is sharp, deliberate, and immersive.
- **Luminescent Accentuation:** Warm amber and radiant yolk-yellow glows serve as heat sources, drawing attention to pivotal interactions, culinary macros, and sensory callouts.
- **Architectural Restraint:** Clean, monoline borders and structured typographic geometry prevent the dark atmosphere from feeling heavy or muddy.
- **Tactile Gastronomy:** Interactive states suggest searing responsiveness—crisp hover transitions, gentle warm ambient blooms, and hyper-legible culinary telemetry.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in dark, light-absorbent culinary metals and minerals, energized by glowing heat elements and rich raw ingredients.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#0C0D0E`):** Pure obsidian void. Serves as the deep stage for all viewports.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#141619`):** Grounded containers, major navigation shelves, and structural drawers.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#1C1F23`):** Raised interactive cards, modals, and overlay sheets.
- **Surface Elevated (`#25292E`):** Floating controls, tooltips, and active popovers.
- **Hairline Border (`#2B3037`):** Low-contrast structural borders providing crisp silhouette separation.

### Culinary Illumination & Accents
- **Primary Saffron (`#F59E0B`):** Core action points, critical CTAs, active tab indicators, and focal selection rings.
- **Secondary Gastronomy Gold (`#EAB308`):** Secondary interactive items, star ratings, and vibrant state toggles.
- **Amber Glow Highlight (`#FBBF24`):** Soft luminous halos, hover indicators, and focal metric numbers.
- **Warm Cream / Light Butter (`#FEF08A`):** High-readability highlights, micro badges, and subtle text emphases against dark charcoal.

### Macro Badge System
Nutritional and flavor telemetry relies on calibrated tonal values that integrate seamlessly against dark surfaces without harsh neon clashes:
- **Protein / Heat (Spiced Ochre):** `#D97706` background with `#FEF3C7` text.
- **Carbohydrates / Starch (Warm Cream):** `#451A03` base with `#FDE68A` text.
- **Fats / Lipids (Golden Saffron):** `#78350F` base with `#FBBF24` text.
- **Fiber / Botanical (Earthy Herb Ochre):** `#2D2A14` base with `#EAB308` text.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs modern culinary tech with precision measurement:

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides structural, slightly technical personality reminiscent of high-end kitchen appliances, experimental menus, and modernist culinary manuals.
- **Narrative & Body (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean legibility and fluid optical balance across dish notes, ingredients, tasting profiles, and cooking steps.
- **Telemetry & Labels (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied exclusively to weights, temperatures, cooking durations, nutritional macros, and status indicators, reinforcing digital kitchen precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored in a responsive 12-column grid for desktop views, transitioning to an 8-column layout for tablets and a 4-column structure for mobile viewports.

### Grid Rhythm
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width containment at `1440px`. Column gap set using `gutter` (24px) and outer viewport padding using `margin` (40px).
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** Fluid grid using `1rem` column gutters and `1.5rem` horizontal margins.
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column compact flow using `gutter-mobile` (12px) and canvas edge pad `margin-mobile` (16px).

### Spacing Application
Internal component spacing follows an arithmetic scale:
- `space-xs` (4px): Metric pill gaps, badge icon-to-text spacing.
- `space-sm` (8px): Form input inner vertical padding, button icon margins.
- `space-md` (16px): Card internal padding, stack intervals between list rows.
- `space-lg` (24px): Inter-section component spacing, header-to-content offsets.
- `space-xl` (40px): Major feature block partitions and container tier spacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical material tiering paired with saffron-tinted ambient glow rather than standard drop shadows.

### Elevation Tiers
1. **Base Zero (`#0C0D0E`):** Viewport canvas. Flat and non-reflective.
2. **Tier 1 - Grounded Cards (`#141619`):** Subtly partitioned with a hairline border (`1px solid #2B3037`).
3. **Tier 2 - Hovered / Active Layers (`#1C1F23`):** Outlined with `1px solid #3F444D` and lifted by a delicate warm ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 16px -2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`.
4. **Tier 3 - Overlays & Popovers (`#25292E`):** Floating sheets and modal menus with `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`.

### Glowing Light Discipline
Amber and saffron glows must remain restrained. Active controls emit a soft radiant border aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`), creating an aesthetic similar to hot induction coils and glowing vacuum tube equipment.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Roundedness Level 2** (`rounded` = `0.5rem` / 8px). This creates an architectural look—reminiscent of stone countertops and metallic culinary gear—while preserving soft, human-friendly ergonomics.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Core Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Buttons, standard text fields, micro badges, and interactive cards.
- **Containers & Panels (`1rem` / 16px):** Outer recipe containers, modular dashboards, and sheet modals.
- **Overlays & Dialogs (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Major culinary viewports and elevated preview overlays.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Metric telemetry tags, status chips, and taste-profile pill tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid saffron background (`#F59E0B`) with deep obsidian text (`#0C0D0E`), bold typography (`Space Grotesk`, medium weight). On hover: shifts to radiant gold (`#FBBF24`) with an amber ambient halo (`0 0 16px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Obsidian surface (`#1C1F23`), border in `#2B3037`, with saffron-tinted text (`#FEF08A`). On hover: border brightens to `#F59E0B` and surface shifts to `#25292E`.
- **Ghost / Minimal:** Transparent background, muted steel text (`#9CA3AF`). On hover: saffron text (`#F59E0B`) with a faint amber highlight glow.

### Chips & Macro Badges
- **Nutritional Macro Badges:** Height of 24px, pill-shaped (`9999px`), styled using `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase. Contains an optional 6px radiant indicator dot colored according to macro category (protein, carb, fat, fiber).
- **Culinary Filter Chips:** Rounded (`0.5rem`), background `#141619`, border `#2B3037`. When selected, fill turns `#1C1F23` with a glowing saffron border (`#F59E0B`) and amber text.

### Cards
- **Recipe & Ingredient Cards:** Grounded `#141619` surface, 1px border (`#2B3037`), 16px internal padding. Image headers feature a subtle gradient falloff (`linear-gradient(to top, #141619, transparent)`) to merge photography into the dark card surface. On hover: card subtly lifts with a saffron ambient glow.

### Form Inputs
- **Fields:** Surface `#141619`, border `#2B3037`, text `#F3F4F6`, placeholder `#6B7280`. Focused state features an amber border (`#F59E0B`) and a soft ring shadow (`0 0 0 2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Unchecked: `#141619` base with a `#3F444D` border.
- Checked: Solid `#F59E0B` fill with `#0C0D0E` checkmark/indicator dot, framed by an amber outer highlight.

### Lists & Tables
- **Ingredient & Step Lists:** Border-bottom separated with `#1C1F23`. Numeric step indicators use `JetBrains Mono` rendered in `#FBBF24`. Hovering highlights the row with background `#141619` and shifts the step accent to `#FEF08A`.
