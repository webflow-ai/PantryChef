Tawa Paneer Bhurji with Multigrain Phulkas & Roasted Jeera Raita

Succulent crumbled paneer tossed on a cast-iron tawa with quick-caramelized onions, bell peppers, fresh palak ribbons, and dry roasted jeera. Paired with soft phulkas and high-probiotic whipped dahi.
